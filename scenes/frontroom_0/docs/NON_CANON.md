# Non-canon additions: Frontrooms 0 - Home

> **Wiki:** <https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/frontrooms> - describes "the Frontrooms" only in
> the abstract (ordinary reality, before no-clipping out of it); it does not describe any specific
> house, character, or event.
> Per [SPEC.md](SPEC.md)'s header, this level's **accuracy estimate is "n/a - this level is
> original content, not canon"**. Unlike `lvl_0`, there is no detailed wiki page to compare this
> level against scene-by-scene, so almost everything here (the house, the player's partner, the
> moving boxes, the dialogue) is original content by construction, not a gap-filling
> `[interpretation]` of a documented fact.

This file still exists, per the project-wide rule in
[CONVENTIONS.md](../../../docs/ai/CONVENTIONS.md#8-tracking-non-canon-additions), for the one
thing this level does share with the wiki: **how** the player leaves it.

| Addition                                   | Why it exists (the gap it fills)                                                                 | Documented in |
|----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------|
| A box, then the player, no-clip through the floor at a specific Y threshold | The wiki confirms no-clipping as *the* way out of the Frontrooms, but documents no specific trigger, location or sequence - this game invents the falling-box-then-player beat and the exact `y`-coordinate thresholds. | [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) §4-5, [SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md) |

**Rule for future contributors:** if this level ever adds a claim that is meant to represent a
documented Frontrooms/Backrooms fact (rather than original house/character content), tag it
